What is HelloKSlang?

HelloKSlang is a free dictionary for K-pop and K-drama slang — the words your bias uses, the terms flooding the comments section, the abbreviations you see everywhere in Korean fan culture but nobody quite explains.

We built it because looking up Korean slang usually means digging through old forum threads or hoping someone answers in a Discord. HelloKSlang is meant to be the shortcut: type the word, get the meaning, the romanization, and a real example sentence — no Korean fluency required.

New slang shows up every week, so the dictionary keeps growing. Heard a word here that's not in it yet? Submit it — every entry gets reviewed by hand before it's added.

HelloKSlang is a small, independent, ad-free project. It isn't affiliated with any entertainment company, idol, or drama.
