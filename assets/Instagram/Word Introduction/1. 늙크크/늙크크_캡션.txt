Today's word: 늙크크 (neuk-keu-keu)

Literally "old creator crew" — a teasing way to call someone out for being behind on the latest meme or trend. It's the sarcastic opposite of being fully in the loop.

You'll see it in comment sections and K-pop fandom chats when someone reacts a little too late to something everyone already knows.
